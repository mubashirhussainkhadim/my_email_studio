# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for Mubashir Hussain. Mubashir is a forward deployed engineer who builds AI agents, WhatsApp automation and software for small businesses. The emails here help small business owners understand what that work can do for them.

## Folders

- **brand/** — Who Mubashir is, what Mubashir offers, and how the emails should sound.
- **leads/** — Notes on the kinds of people and businesses the emails are for.
- **campaigns/** — Campaign plans and the email drafts that belong to each one.
- **notes/** — Research, decisions and anything else worth keeping.

## Rules

1. **Write in plain English.** Use short sentences and everyday words. Avoid jargon and hype.
2. **Never send email to real people.** Only write drafts. Do not send, schedule or test-send any email to a real address.
3. **Ask me before deleting anything.** This includes files, folders and text inside a file.
