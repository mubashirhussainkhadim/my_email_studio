# Campaign plan: AI-era pricing launch

Written 8 November 2026. Prices and offers come from [brand/offers.md](../../brand/offers.md). Tone and rules come from [brand/voice.md](../../brand/voice.md).

## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name (inside the studio):** AI-era pricing launch

**Name readers see:** "New fixed prices". In the emails, say "fixed prices" or "new prices", not "AI-era pricing". The plain phrase is clearer and doesn't sound like a sales pitch.

**Summary:** Tell the people who have already been in touch that Mubashir now has fixed, published prices for six kinds of work, and invite them to a free 30-minute call to find out what their job would cost.

**Main goal:** 30 people from the list book a free 30-minute call between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.

- A call counts when it is booked in that window, even if the call itself happens later in January.
- 30 out of about 500 people is 6% of the list. That is a big ask for email alone, so the plan puts most of the effort into the first two weeks, before the holidays.

**Other goals:**

- People who are not ready for a call yet learn that the prices are on the website and that they can try a demo first.
- Get clean numbers on which group books calls best, to plan the next campaign.
- Keep unsubscribes low. The list is small and hard to replace.

## 2. Who it's for

About 500 people who have been in touch through the contact form, a past call booking or the newsletter. They know Mubashir's name. They are busy, not technical, and want to know what the work will do and what it will cost.

Before the campaign starts, write down how many people are in each group. The targets in section 7 use those numbers.

| Group | Who they are | What worries them | What moves them | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ai-curious** | Exploring what an AI agent could do. Have never hired a developer. | "I don't know what's possible." "I'll get a big bill I didn't expect." "I'll look silly asking basic questions." | Simple examples from businesses like theirs. A demo they can try alone. A starting price they can see. | Early: still learning |
| **comparing-devs** | Weighing a typical AI website or chatbot builder against hiring an expert. | "Is an expert worth the money?" "Will a person cost more and take longer?" "Will the builder actually work with my systems?" | A fair, plain comparison. Clear prices. Knowing someone will connect it to their real systems and stay around after launch. | Middle: comparing options |
| **ready-to-scope** | Know roughly what they want built. Want a fixed price. | "The price will grow once work starts." "I'll be stuck with someone who disappears." | A written, fixed quote before any work starts. A clear list of what each package includes. A quick reply. | Late: ready to decide |

**Where they are:** their email inbox. Everyone on the list has already given their email address to Mubashir, so email is the only channel in this plan.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message (one sentence):**
Mubashir's prices are now fixed and written down before any work starts, so book a free 30-minute call to find out what your job would cost.

**Supporting messages:**

1. **You know the price before work starts.** Every job has a fixed price, agreed in writing. No surprises.
   - Proof: the price list on mubashirhussain.com/services, and the written quote every client gets before work starts.
2. **You can see where prices start.** Six kinds of work, each from $600 (custom web apps and websites from $900). The final price is a written, fixed quote once Mubashir understands the problem.
   - Proof: the packages in brand/offers.md, which match the website.
3. **It works inside your business, safely.** An agent can check an order, look up a price or book a free slot. A person approves anything sensitive, such as refunds, payments or deletions. Every action is logged.
   - Proof: the ground rules in brand/offers.md. The demos at mubashirhussain.com/demos (sample agents on sample data, nothing is saved or sent).
4. **Mubashir doesn't disappear after launch.** Mubashir works inside the business with you.
   - Proof: the ground rules in brand/offers.md. Only mention support, training or documentation when the package named includes them.

**The angle for each group:**

| Group | Main angle | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| ai-curious | Start small and see it first. | "Try a sample agent on our demos page. Nothing is saved or sent. If you'd like one for your business, a single-task agent starts from $600." |
| comparing-devs | A fair comparison, then a clear price. | "A builder can be a good fit for a simple chat box. If it needs to look up orders or book slots in your own systems, that's what I build, for a fixed price." |
| ready-to-scope | The steps to a fixed quote. | "Tell me what you need on a 30-minute call. I'll send a written, fixed price. The timeline is agreed in writing before work starts." |

**Things every email must avoid:**

- No guaranteed results, and no promises of more sales, customers or money.
- No build times or delivery dates. Say "the timeline is agreed in writing before work starts."
- Always "from $600", never just "$600", unless naming a package.
- Don't name or knock other builders or developers. Explain what Mubashir does and let the reader compare.
- No client stories, numbers or quotes unless they are real and the client has said yes. There are none in the studio yet, so don't make any up.
- No hype words (revolutionary, game-changer, leverage, synergy) and no tech terms without a plain explanation.

## 7. How we'll measure it

**Main number:** calls booked from campaign emails. Target: 30 by Sunday 3 January 2027.

**Weekly targets** (front-loaded because of the holidays):

| End of | Calls booked (running total) |
|---|---|
| Week 1 (Sun 13 Dec) | 10 |
| Week 2 (Sun 20 Dec) | 22 |
| Week 3 (Sun 27 Dec) | 26 |
| Week 4 (Sun 3 Jan) | 30 |

**How the numbers could add up (a planning guess, not a benchmark):** if about 150 people (30% of the list) click a link at least once during the campaign, and 1 in 5 of them books, that's 30 calls. If clicks are much lower after week 1, that is the early warning.

**Other numbers to watch:**

| Number | Why it matters | How to track it |
|---|---|---|
| Calls booked per group | Shows which group responds best | A different tracking link per group and per email (see below) |
| Clicks to /book, per email | Shows which emails work | The email tool's click report |
| Share of /book visitors who book | Shows whether the booking page itself puts people off | Booking tool, plus website visits from the tracking links |
| Replies | Some people reply instead of booking | Count replies in the inbox, and how many turn into calls |
| Unsubscribes per email | Warns if we're sending too much. Aim to keep it under 0.5% per email. | The email tool's report |
| Opens | A rough guide only. Some email apps open emails automatically, so the number isn't reliable. | The email tool's report |

**Tracking links:** add tags to every /book, /services and /demos link, for example:

`https://mubashirhussain.com/book?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=ai-era-pricing&utm_content=e2-ready-to-scope`

If the booking tool can't read these tags, add one question to the booking form: "What made you book this call?"

**Reporting:** a short count each week: Friday 11 December, Friday 18 December, Wednesday 23 December (moved from Friday 25 December, which is Christmas Day) and Friday 1 January, and a final results note after 3 January. The `/marketing:performance-report` skill can help write it.

## 9. Risks and what to do about them

**1. The holidays cut the campaign to about two real weeks.**
Most bookings need to happen in weeks 1 and 2. Send four of the six emails before 18 December. Let people book calls for early January, and count them if they book before 3 January. If the count is short on 3 January, decide whether to keep the booking link open one more week and count those bookings separately.

**2. 30 calls from 500 people is a big ask for email alone.**
Check on Friday 18 December. If there are fewer than 15 bookings: send ready-to-scope non-bookers a short, personal plain-text note from Mubashir asking what they'd like built, and make the reply option more prominent in E5 and E6. Don't add more emails than planned. Sending too much will cost unsubscribes.

**3. An email slips into hype or makes a promise we can't keep.**
Price emails can drift into "save money" or "more sales" lines, or a delivery date. Run `/marketing:brand-review` on every draft against brand/voice.md and brand/offers.md before Mubashir approves it.

**4. Some people on the list never agreed to get marketing emails.**
People who used the contact form or booked a call may not have signed up for the newsletter. Before sending, check what each person agreed to. Every email needs an unsubscribe link and the footer details the email tool requires (such as a postal address). Send from Mubashir's real address so replies reach Mubashir.

**5. Not enough call slots, or slow replies.**
If 15 people try to book in week 1 and find no slots, they may not come back. Open enough slots for weeks 1 and 2 before E1, and reply to emails within one working day.
