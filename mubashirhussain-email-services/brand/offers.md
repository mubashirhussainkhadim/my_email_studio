# Offers

What Mubashir builds, and what it costs. Source: https://mubashirhussain.com/services (checked 2026-11-08). If the site changes, update this file.

## How pricing works

- Every job has a **fixed price** in USD, agreed in writing before any work starts.
- The prices below are **starting prices**. The final price is a written, fixed quote once Mubashir understands the problem.
- Hourly work is $25 to $49 an hour. The smallest project is $600.
- Meta charges WhatsApp conversation fees straight to the client's account. Mubashir does not mark them up.

**Rules for emails:**
- Always say "from $600", never just "$600", unless you mean a named package.
- The site gives **no build times**. Never promise a delivery date or a number of days. Say "the timeline is agreed in writing before work starts."
- Explain technical names in plain words. For example, say "connect your systems so data moves by itself", not "CRM/ERP integration".

## 1. AI agent or chatbot (from $600)

An agent that does real tasks in your systems: checks an order, looks up a price, finds a free slot and books it. A person approves anything sensitive, such as refunds, payments or deletions.

- **Single-task agent, $600:** one agent, one task (for example answering product or policy questions), one data source.
- **Multi-tool agent, $2,000:** up to three tools (lookup, booking, quote), an approval step for sensitive actions and a chat box on your website.
- **Agent with team handover, $3,000:** several tools and data sources, approval flow, action log, staff dashboard, written handover and a 30-minute training call.

## 2. WhatsApp Business and AI assistant (from $600)

Sets up the official WhatsApp Business API, then adds a shared inbox and an assistant that hands over to a person when needed.

- **Connect and verify, $600:** Meta app and number setup, and one test flow.
- **Inbox and assistant, $1,750:** the above, plus a shared inbox, an AI assistant that answers your FAQs and up to three approved message templates.
- **Full automation, $2,500:** the above, plus opt-in broadcasts with STOP handling, booking or order lookup, a switch for a person to take over and delivery reports.

## 3. Connect Claude or ChatGPT to your systems (from $600)

Lets AI assistants read your data and act in your systems, with only the permissions you choose. (Technical name: MCP server.)

- **Read-only connector, $600:** one system, up to three read tools, documentation.
- **Read and write, $1,750:** one system, up to six tools including create and update, per-user permissions and a log of every action.
- **Multi-system connector, $2,500:** up to two systems, up to ten tools, per-user permissions, human approval for sensitive actions, tests and deployment help.

## 4. Connect your business systems (from $600)

Connects your CRM, booking, payment tools, spreadsheets or custom software so data moves by itself.

- **One connection, $600:** two systems, one direction, with an error log.
- **Two-way sync, $1,750:** data moves both ways, with retries, alerts and written rules.
- **Multi-system workflow, $2,500:** up to three systems, two-way sync, scheduled jobs, monitoring and full documentation.

## 5. AI workflow automation (from $600)

Takes over repeat daily tasks such as reading documents, chasing replies, entering data and sorting leads. A person approves risky steps.

- **One workflow, $600:** one automated workflow and one input type, tested on your samples.
- **Workflow with approvals, $1,750:** the above, plus an approval step, notifications, error handling and a log.
- **End-to-end process, $2,500:** up to three connected steps, approvals, a dashboard of runs, documentation and a handover call.

## 6. Custom web app, booking system or website (from $900)

Booking systems, point-of-sale, customer portals, dashboards and business websites. Working progress every week.

- **Small app or site, $900:** up to five pages or screens, works on phones, a contact or booking form and a basic admin.
- **Business app, $3,000:** up to twelve screens, user accounts and roles, an admin dashboard, email notifications and one payment or other system connection.
- **Full platform, $5,000:** several user roles, booking or ordering, payments, reports, up to three connections, tests and deployment support.

## Ground rules on every job

- Agents never make up data.
- Payments, refunds, discounts and deletions wait for a person to confirm.
- An agent only gets the permissions of the person it serves, and every action is logged.
- Mubashir works inside the business, and doesn't disappear after launch.

Support, training and documentation come with some packages, not all. Check the package before mentioning them.
