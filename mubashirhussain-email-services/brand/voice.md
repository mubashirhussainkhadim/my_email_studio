# Brand Voice Guide

Who we write for: small business owners, and anyone else who needs AI agents, WhatsApp automation or software built for them. They are busy. They are not technical. They want to know what the work will do for them and what it will cost.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Trusted. Plain. Honest.**

- **Trusted** — The reader should finish the email feeling they can rely on Mubashir. This matters more than anything else.
- **Plain** — Short sentences and everyday words. Write it the way you would explain it to a shop owner over tea.
- **Honest** — Say what the work does and what it costs. Don't stretch the truth.

## 2. Words we use

- **fixed price**
- **no surprises**
- **I'll build it**

These words come from how Mubashir works: the price is written down before any work starts. Use them where they fit. Don't force them into every line.

## 3. Words we avoid

- revolutionary
- game-changer
- leverage
- synergy

The same goes for any other hype or jargon. If a word sounds like a sales pitch or a tech conference, use a simpler one.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **Guaranteed results.** Never say or suggest that results are guaranteed.
- **Promising extra sales.** Never promise more sales, more customers or more money, and never put a number on it (for example, "double your sales").

We can describe what the work does, such as "your WhatsApp replies to customers at night". We do not promise what it will earn.
